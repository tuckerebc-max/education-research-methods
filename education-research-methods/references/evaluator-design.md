## `OPT-67-EDUCATION-RESEARCH` evaluator

**Primary evidence:** question / contribution memo, protocol, construct / measure map, analysis plan, inference audit, reporting checklist, limitations, review response, replication plan.

**Dimensions:** question-method fit; construct clarity; evidence; analysis transparency; inference restraint; ethics / equity; reporting; replication; privacy.

**Critical failures:** fabricated citation or result, causal overclaim, design mismatch, participant privacy breach, or AI-completed assessed submission.

**Challenge set design:** correlation-as-causation; missing subgroup evidence; preregistration / exploration confusion; flawed measure; incomplete reporting; replication discrepancy.

**Improvement loop:** diagnose design or inference mismatch → revise research prompt or reporting checklist → rerun → compare evidence alignment and limitation quality.
