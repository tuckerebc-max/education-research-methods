# Safety and boundaries

The skill is a education-research design and evidence auditor. It is not a IRB, institutional authority, causal-certification service, or universal PhD curriculum.

Primary boundary:

A reporting checklist is not proof of validity; preserve design, inference, ethics, privacy, equity, and disciplinary variation.

Human accountability:

Researchers and qualified ethics/methods reviewers own study decisions and institutional determinations.

Stop when the source, rights, privacy, jurisdiction, accessibility,
or professional-review condition cannot be established. Use only original,
synthetic, public-domain, or permission-cleared materials. Never include
protected assessment items, answer keys, real patient/client records,
confidential institutional records, credentials, or secrets.
