# `OPT-67-EDUCATION-RESEARCH` textbook-skill architecture

**Architecture ID:** `F2-ARCH-67-EDUCATION-RESEARCH-001`  
**Status:** `SPEC_DRAFT — COMPOSITE`  
**Promise:** Teach research design, evidence, reporting, critique, and communication as a transparent practice rather than a formula.

## Learner change

Before: the learner treats a literature review, method, or result as proof.  
After: the learner aligns question, design, measure, evidence, inference, reporting, ethics, and audience while exposing uncertainty.

## Progression

`Question → Design → Define → Analyze → Interpret → Report → Review → Replicate / improve`

## Unit map

| Unit | Work | Learner evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Researchable question and contribution | question / contribution memo |
| 2 | Design, population, data, and measures | protocol and construct map |
| 3 | Analysis plan and evidence | analysis / data audit |
| 4 | Validity, uncertainty, equity, and consequences | inference and limitations memo |
| 5 | Transparent reporting and peer review | manuscript reporting audit |
| 6 | Replication, sensitivity, and improvement | revision / rerun plan |

## Source boundary

AERA, AERA / APA / NCME, APA JARS, and QAA provide complementary field-level standards and benchmarks. They do not form one universal PhD curriculum; doctoral exemplars must remain labeled.

## Human-plus-agent design

The agent may organize literature and audit reporting completeness. The human owns question, design, interpretation, ethics, source verification, and participant protection. Agent-generated synthesis must remain a draft until checked against primary sources.

## Evaluator seed

Dimensions: question-method fit, construct clarity, evidence traceability, analysis transparency, uncertainty, ethics / equity, reporting completeness, inference restraint, and replication readiness.

## First drafting packet

Draft `EDRES-001` will repair a plausible but flawed education-research protocol, then require a transparent reporting plan and an explicit list of claims the study cannot support.
