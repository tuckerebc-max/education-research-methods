## `OPT-67-EDUCATION-RESEARCH` — Repair a plausible but flawed study

**Learner target:** Align question, design, measure, evidence, inference, reporting, and ethics.

**Fictional protocol:** A school compares two instructional approaches using volunteer classrooms, a locally written outcome measure, and a single posttest. The draft claims the preferred approach causes higher achievement.

**Learner task:** Identify the research question and causal claim, audit the design and measure, list alternative explanations, specify missing reporting information, narrow the conclusion, and propose a better next study.

**Required artifact:** question / contribution memo, design map, construct / measure map, inference audit, reporting checklist, limitations and revision plan.

**Nonmodel:** Treating a reporting checklist as proof that the causal claim is valid.  
**Feedback move:** Ask “Which part of the claim is supported, which part is not, and what design change would reduce the uncertainty?”

**Agent handoff:** Agent may audit completeness and propose alternatives; researcher owns design, interpretation, ethics, and reporting.

**Exit test:** Add a subgroup with different baseline conditions. Learner must address subgroup evidence and equity before interpreting the result.
